Identity

Turning Data into Knowledge

The Data Science Laboratory is an academic research unit of the School of Production Engineering and Management at the Technical University of Crete.

Building on a long-established background in data analysis and forecasting, the laboratory has evolved into a contemporary research environment focused on Data Science, Business Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, and intelligent decision support systems. Our mission is to transform data into actionable knowledge and support informed decision-making in academia, industry, and society.

Implementation

Applied Research & Industry Impact

Turning data into knowledge and real-world impact

The Data Science Laboratory bridges academic excellence with practical implementation, delivering advanced analytical solutions for organizations, institutions, and industry partners. Our applied research focuses on the development of robust methodologies, predictive models, intelligent decision-support systems, and data-driven tools that address complex challenges across banking, finance, aviation, tourism, sustainability, operations, and public policy.

Featured Areas of Applied Impact

Artificial Intelligence risk management methodologies for banking and financial institutions

Forecasting models for business and operational planning

Aviation and airport sustainability analytics

Tourism intelligence and destination performance monitoring

Data-driven sustainability assessment tools

Intelligent systems for organizational and policy decision support

Media contributions and analytical publications across digital platforms

Participation in applied projects and external collaborations

Research Approach

The laboratory adopts a multidisciplinary approach, combining Data Science, Business Analytics, AI/ML, and econometric analysis with selected qualitative and field-based research approaches, including ethnographic inquiry when relevant. Our projects are actively led by a dynamic team, ensuring both scientific rigor and practical relevance.

Industry Collaboration

Alpha Bank – High-Impact Strategic Projects

Dimitris Zopounidis serves as Lead Researcher on strategic projects developed in collaboration with Alpha Bank, focusing on Artificial Intelligence, risk management, data-driven decision-making and regional economic development.

His work includes the development of Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Methodologies in alignment with the EU AI Act, supporting governance frameworks, risk assessment processes and decision-support mechanisms for financial institutions.

He also serves as Principal Investigator, Lead Researcher and Project Coordinator of a large-scale data-driven regional economic analysis project for Crete, conducted in collaboration with Alpha Bank to support evidence-based strategy and decision-making.

In parallel, he contributes to initiatives that strengthen the strategic relationship between Alpha Bank and the Technical University of Crete through collaborative activities associated with the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the two organizations.
